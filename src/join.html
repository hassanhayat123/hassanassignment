Get involved

How to Join a Club

Registration takes about ten minutes and is open to every enrolled student. There is no membership fee for your first society, and you can attend one meeting as a guest before you commit.

Five steps to membership

Follow these in order. Steps one to four happen online; step five is your first meeting on campus.

  1. 1

    Pick a club

    Read through the club categories and shortlist one or two societies. First-years are strongly advised to start with a single club — you can always add another next semester.

  2. 2

    Fill in the registration form

    Collect the membership form from the Student Affairs Office or download it from the student portal. You will need your name, roll number, department, semester and university email address.

  3. 3

    Submit it to the club office bearer

    Hand the completed form to the club President or General Secretary. Every club keeps a desk at the Student Activities Centre during the first two weeks of the semester.

  4. 4

    Wait for confirmation

    You will receive a confirmation email on your university address within three working days, along with the club's meeting schedule and the contact details of your assigned mentor.

  5. 5

    Attend your first meeting

    Turn up to the next weekly session. The first meeting is an orientation — you will be introduced to the committee, walked through the semester plan, and asked which sub-team you would like to work with.

Participation guidelines

Short and reasonable. Clubs are student-run, so these exist mainly so that everyone knows what to expect from each other.

Attendance

Attend at least 60% of weekly meetings across the semester to stay on the active roll and be eligible for the club certificate.

Notice for absence

Tell your team lead in advance if you cannot make a session, especially in the two weeks before an event you are assigned to.

Respect and conduct

The university code of conduct applies at every club activity, on campus and off. Harassment of any kind means immediate removal.

Shared resources

Equipment, rooms and budgets belong to the club. Book them through the committee and return anything you borrow in working order.

Academics come first

If club work is affecting your coursework, tell your mentor. Reduced duties during exam weeks are normal and always granted.

Leaving a club

You can withdraw at any time by informing the General Secretary in writing. Hand back any club property before you go.

Ready to sign up?

Collect a membership form from the Student Affairs Office, or drop by any club desk at the Student Activities Centre during the first two weeks of term.
