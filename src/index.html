Why student clubs matter

Lectures teach you a subject. Clubs teach you everything around it — how to lead a team, run a budget, speak in front of a room, and build something with people who are not on your course. Employers notice it, and so will you.

01

Skills you cannot cram

Leadership, public speaking, event planning and teamwork are learned by doing. A society gives you a low-risk place to practise all four before they matter in an interview.

02

A network across years

Clubs mix first-years with final-years and alumni. That is how juniors find mentors, how internships get referred, and how project teams form outside your immediate class.

03

Balance that keeps you going

A structured hour of sport, music or debate each week is one of the most reliable ways students protect their focus and their wellbeing across a long semester.
