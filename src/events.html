This semester

Upcoming Events

Everything student clubs have scheduled between September and December. Most sessions are open to all students — a few competitions need you to register with the organising club first.

Event calendar

Sorted by date. Anything marked Registration required closes one week before the event.

  • 12 Sep
    Workshop

    Intro to Git & GitHub for Beginners

    A hands-on two-hour session covering branching, pull requests and how to recover when you break something. Bring a laptop.

    Computing Society CS Lab 3, Block B 3:00 – 5:00 PM

  • 24 Sep
    Competition

    Inter-Departmental Cricket Cup — Opening Round

    Eight departmental sides, knockout format, running across three weekends. Squad lists must be submitted to the Sports Office beforehand.

    Cricket Club Main Ground Registration required

  • 08 Oct
    Seminar

    Careers in Artificial Intelligence

    A panel of four alumni working in machine learning talk through how they got in, what they actually do, and what they wish they had studied.

    AI & Data Circle Auditorium A 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM

  • 19 Oct
    Meetup

    Autumn Photo Walk — Old City

    A guided walk through the old quarter with a short composition briefing first. Any camera, including a phone, is fine.

    Photography Circle Meet at Main Gate 7:30 AM – 12:00 PM

  • 02 Nov
    Competition

    National Debating Championship — Campus Qualifiers

    British Parliamentary format. The two winning teams represent the university at the national circuit in January.

    English Debating Society Seminar Hall 2 Registration required

  • 15 Nov
    Workshop

    Robotics Build Night — Line Follower Sprint

    Teams of three build and race a line-following robot in a single evening. All components and tools are provided by the club.

    Robotics Club Engineering Workshop 5:00 – 10:00 PM

  • 06 Dec
    Cultural

    Winter Concert & Drama Night

    The Music Club and Dramatics Society close the semester with a joint evening — a short play followed by a live set.

    Music Club & Dramatics Society Open Air Theatre 6:00 PM onwards
